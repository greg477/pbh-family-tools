Simple tools that make the good stuff add up.

Three phone-based reward systems, each built for a different age — grounded in the same behavioral science we use every day, and designed to stay out of your way. Pick the one that fits your kid, add it to your home screen, and you're running in a minute.

⭐ Built on positive reinforcement ● Your data stays on your device 🎁 Fully customizable
Tools that grow with your child
Little kids · roughly ages 3–8

Points & Prize Jar

A reward jar that fills with coins as your child earns stars for everyday jobs, then empties when they trade for a prize.

How it works: tap a job to add points · watch the jar fill toward the next prize · trade points at the prize shelf. Earn-only, no point loss.

Game Time

Screen time becomes something earned through the day, with a live countdown when it's time to play.

How it works: earn minutes for routines and chores · lose a few for missed tasks · tap Start to play and watch the clock tick down. Includes a daily cap.

Teens · roughly ages 13+

Weekly Allowance

The whole week's allowance is given up front; small fines come off for broken rules, and good choices earn some back.

How it works: allowance front-loaded Monday · small fines (with a daily cap) for agreed rules · bonuses for genuine repair · syncs across both parents' phones and a teen view.

Getting it on your phone

Each tool is a web page that installs like an app — no app store, nothing to pay for.

📱 iPhone & iPad

  1. Open the tool's link in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button (the square with an up arrow).
  3. Tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.

🤖 Android

  1. Open the tool's link in Chrome.
  2. Tap the ⋮ menu (three dots).
  3. Tap Add to Home screen, then Add.
Your family's privacy: these tools keep everything on your own device. Nothing about your child is collected, sent to us, or shared — with one exception you turn on yourself: the teen allowance tool can sync between two parents' phones using your own private cloud setup, described in its setup guide.
